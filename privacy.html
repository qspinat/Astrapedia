Privacy Policy

Astrapedia — Last updated: March 2026

Astrapedia is a free, offline star map application. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

Location

Astrapedia may request access to your device location. Location data is used solely to calculate the position of stars, planets, and constellations relative to your local horizon. It is:

  • Processed entirely on your device
  • Never stored persistently beyond your current session
  • Never transmitted to any server or third party

You can use the app without granting location access by entering coordinates manually.

Data collection

Astrapedia does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There are no user accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no advertising.

Network access

The app may make network requests to fetch optional content such as astronomical images from public sources (ESA/Hubble, NASA, Wikimedia Commons). No personal data is included in these requests.

Third-party services

No third-party SDKs, analytics tools, or advertising networks are included in this app.

Children

Astrapedia does not knowingly collect any information from users of any age.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the app's data practices change. The latest version is always available at this URL.
